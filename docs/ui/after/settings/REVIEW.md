The scrolled mobile invitation view exposes the empty-state copy; its primary/muted text is legible against the neutral surface. The token pairs are covered by existing contrast tests and DESIGN.md. Offscreen/clipped nodes cannot be certified by a screenshot. Copilot can overlap the bottom metadata line in the360 read-only capture; scrolling reveals it, but relocating the shared trigger is deferred.
- `aria-hidden-focus`: Radix marks the background app shell and its focus sentinels aria-hidden while a dialog is open. The browser suite proves Escape and focus return. Additional role-dialog Tab/Shift+Tab boundary assertions pass in the targeted dark1440 focus test, rather than treating the incomplete flag as automatically passing. This does not establish every provider/browser/screen-reader behavior or programmatic focus prevention.
- The light390 revocation capture shows the shell skip link above the scrim and a success toast behind the modal. Its visual stacking and the other dialogs' full manual focus review remain unresolved shared-overlay polish. Do not describe all incomplete items as closed or the page as fully WCAG-certified.
